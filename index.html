// ABOUT

I'm an embedded systems engineer at Techmology, where for the past 3+ years I've taken hardware products from concept to production: schematic capture, PCB layout, component selection, firmware, board bring-up, and the lab-bench debugging that turns a prototype into something that actually ships. I also lead product and development there, so I'm used to client-facing outcomes as well as hands-on engineering.

Day to day that means designing educational robotics and IoT hardware end to end, and writing firmware for STM32 and ESP32 boards: motor control, sensor acquisition, and communication over UART, I²C, SPI and CAN. I'm also a doctoral researcher at the University of Blida 1, in partnership with CDTA, working on low-power RISC-V SoC design. That gives me a technical edge when your project touches FPGAs or custom silicon.

// FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside Algeria?

Yes. I work fully remotely with clients abroad, in English or French.

What exactly will I receive?

Native design files (KiCad or Altium), Gerbers, BOM and pick-and-place files, firmware source in a git repo, and short documentation with bring-up notes.

Who owns the design files?

You do. You get the full source on delivery.

Can you fix or review an existing board or firmware?

Yes. I take on bring-up, debugging and design reviews of existing hardware and firmware.

Can you get the boards manufactured?

I prepare fab- and assembly-ready files for JLCPCB and PCBWay, and can help with ordering.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes.

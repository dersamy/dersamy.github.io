Soil Quality Measurement System

A remote soil-monitoring board built around an Atmega328P, GSM backhaul, and an RS485 soil sensor.

// The brief

Soil monitoring in the field means no easy access to Wi-Fi or wired networking, and often a sensor bus that has to run over a longer cable than typical hobby wiring. Both push the design toward cellular backhaul and a more robust physical layer than plain I²C or UART.

// What I built

A 2-layer PCB pairing an Atmega328P with a SIM800L GSM module for connectivity, reading the soil sensor over RS485, which tolerates longer runs and noisier environments far better than single-ended serial. A meaningful part of the effort went into power delivery and signal integrity, keeping communication stable over distance on a small, power-constrained board.

// Result

A built and running board that keeps long-distance RS485 data acquisition stable with a GSM uplink and no local network.
